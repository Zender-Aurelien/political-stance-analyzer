Civic Lens

Know your representatives.
Know your choices.

A nonpartisan tool for New Yorkers who want to understand where their elected officials stand and whether those positions align with their own values.

Our Mission

Closing the gap between voters and their government

Most New Yorkers can name their mayor or governor. Far fewer know their state assemblymember, their city council rep, or what any of them actually stand for on the issues that affect daily life.

Civic Lens exists to close that gap. We surface candidate profiles, policy positions, and verifiable sources so that anyone, regardless of political background, can make a more informed choice at the ballot box.

We don't tell you who to vote for. We give you the information to decide for yourself.

New York officials in action. Civic Lens profiles federal, state, and local representatives.

How It Works

Three steps to your match

  1. 01

    Search your area

    Enter your ZIP code or a candidate's name. Civic Lens surfaces the elected officials and candidates active in your district, from U.S. Senate down to your local assembly seat.

  2. 02

    Take the values quiz

    Answer a short set of questions about the issues that matter to you: housing, healthcare, climate, taxes, and more. Every question is framed neutrally with context on both sides of the issue. All questions are optional.

  3. 03

    See your results

    Civic Lens calculates a match percentage for each candidate based on how closely their documented positions align with your answers. You'll also see which candidates represent your specific district.

Civic Participation

New Yorkers show up

From city council hearings to statewide marches, New Yorkers have always been engaged. Civic Lens is built for that energy, giving residents the tools to channel it into informed political participation.

Data & Sources

Where the data comes from

Candidate profiles are drawn from official government sources including the U.S. House Clerk, Senate records, the New York State Board of Elections, and official campaign and government websites. Each profile links to its source directly.

Profiles marked "Preview" are layout samples that may not reflect a current election lineup and have not been fully verified. We note verification dates where available.

Your privacy

Civic Lens stores nothing on a server. Your quiz answers, ZIP code, and match results live only in your browser's localStorage. No account is required. No data is ever transmitted or sold.

Clearing your browser data removes all stored information instantly.

Elected representatives address constituents at a public press conference. Civic Lens links directly to official government profiles and verified sources.

Civic Lens is an independent civic technology project built for the IBM SkillsBuild competition. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or funded by any political candidate, party, or government agency. Match percentages are algorithmic estimates based on publicly available information and user responses and are not political endorsements. Always consult official sources before making voting decisions.
