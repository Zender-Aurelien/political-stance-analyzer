Civic Lens helps New Yorkers learn about political candidates, explore their experience, and find sources to better understand their choices. This version uses sample profiles stored in a local data file, with links to official government and election websites for further research. The profiles are previews, not a verified election lineup. Live candidate data and ZIP-based district lookup are still being developed.
